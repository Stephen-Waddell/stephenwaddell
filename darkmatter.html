"When Stephen Waddell paused to look at a dark, shadowy form on the wall of an underground parking garage, and made the picture Stain (2012) at the site, he drew upon an ancient tradition of deriving ideas from “chance images.”3 Stain redirected Waddell’s imagination, and the underground car park became the antechamber to Dark Matter Atlas, the first substantial body of work in black and white for an artist best known for his distinctive use of colour photography."

- Section taken from "Underworlds" essay by Craig Burnett
