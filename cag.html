"This publication, produced by the Contemporary Art Gallery in 2008, accompanied the exhibition by Stephen Waddell at the Contemporary Art Gallery, April 4 – June 1, 2008. The exhibition was a survey of the artist’s paintings, photographic studies and films ranging from 1995 to 2008 and was curated by renowned artist Roy Arden. The publication includes a foreword by Christina Ritchie and critical texts written by Katrin Blum and Roy Arden with colour reproductions of 25 artworks."

- Contemporary Art Gallery
